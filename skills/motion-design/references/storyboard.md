## 1. The table

```md
| Time | On screen | Motion | Scene exit | Continuity object |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2.0 | … | … | … | … |
```

- Put the times on a beat grid (120 BPM is every 0.5 s): you feel cuts and accents on the beat even without music.
- "Scene exit" is a specific move from `references/concept.md`, section 4, not the word "transition".
- Mark where the hook is, where the surprises are (every 5–8 s), where the climax is and where the rest is.
- Text stays on screen for at least `words × 0.3 s + 1 s`.

## 3. Review before the render

Go through this list on the contact sheet of stills before you start the full render. Fix every "no" or explain it in the reply.

1. The first frame has content, and after 2 s it's clear what the video is about.
2. The direction passes the swap test: another model would not make almost the same thing from a similar brief.
3. There is a signature moment built from a brand element.
4. One continuity object links at least two cuts.
5. Something unexpected happens every 5–8 s: a change of scale, colour, perspective or direction.
6. Every transition has a reason: a shape, colour, direction or object.
7. Each shot has one main action, and the background stays quiet.
8. The eye doesn't jump: new information appears where the eye already is, or the motion leads the eye to it.
9. Text: one thought per shot, at most 7 words, on screen for at least words × 0.3 s + 1 s, readable on a phone.
10. The product is shown in action on one concrete example.
11. There is a climax (about 70–80% of the video) and a rest at the end.
12. The end card stays for at least 2–3 s, closes the motif from the opening, and everything on it is true.
